Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about our incubator program, funding, and application process.

Who can apply for the incubator program?

We primarily look for early-stage tech startups, especially in SaaS, AI, Web3, and FinTech. You should ideally have a working Minimum Viable Product (MVP) or be in the beta testing phase with some early user traction. Both solo founders and teams with complimentary skills are highly encouraged to apply.

What is the duration of the program?

The core intensive program runs for exactly 12 weeks. During this time, you will have weekly sprint reviews, mentorship sessions, and product teardowns. After the initial 3 months, you will graduate at our Demo Day, followed by an additional 6 months of extended alumni support and investor networking access.

Is funding guaranteed upon joining?

While funding is not 100% guaranteed, we provide you with all the tools, metrics, and investor connections needed to close your round. Startups that successfully complete our program and hit their growth milestones have an 85% success rate in raising a seed round within 3 months of Demo Day. Plus, you get over $100k in free cloud credits and partner perks immediately.

Do you take equity in the startups?

Yes, for our standard accelerator track, we take a 5% to 7% equity stake in exchange for our initial pre-seed capital, unlimited mentorship, office space, and lifetime network access. However, we also offer an equity-free "Bootstrapped Track" where you pay a flat tuition fee instead of giving up shares in your company.

Can international founders apply?

Absolutely! We are a remote-friendly, global incubator. We have successfully backed founders from over 20 different countries. If you are an international founder looking to target global markets, we also provide legal assistance for incorporating your company as a Delaware C-Corp and navigating founder visa processes.
