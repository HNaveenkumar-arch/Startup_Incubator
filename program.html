Program Roadmap

Your step-by-step journey from application to graduation. We guide you at every stage.

📝

1. Apply

Submit your startup details, pitch deck, and founder profiles through our online portal.

Ensure all documents are up-to-date and clearly highlight your core value proposition.

Early applicants get priority review and faster feedback from the selection committee.

🔍

2. Review

Our expert panel evaluates your idea based on market potential, innovation, and team capability.

Feedback will be provided within 2-3 weeks of your application submission.

We specifically look for strong problem-solving capabilities and excellent team synergy.

🎯

3. Selection

Shortlisted startups will be invited for a virtual or in-person interview before receiving the final offer.

Interviews will be conducted directly by our core advisory board and managing partners.

Final selection is heavily based on scalability, market readiness, and execution speed.

🤝

4. Mentorship

Get paired with industry veterans and domain experts for 1-on-1 guidance and strategy planning.

Weekly sprint meetings will be scheduled to keep your product progress on track.

You will also get exclusive access to specialized legal, financial, and marketing advisors.

⚙️

5. Product Development

Access our labs, free AWS credits, and tech architects to build and refine your Minimum Viable Product.

Our in-house UI/UX experts will help refine your user journeys for maximum retention.

Rigorous beta testing phases will be implemented to ensure a bug-free public launch.

🎤

6. Demo Day

Pitch your refined product in front of top-tier Venture Capitalists, angel investors, and media.

Rehearse your presentation multiple times with professional communication coaches beforehand.

Network heavily with industry leaders and potential partners at our exclusive after-party.

🎓

7. Graduation

Join our elite alumni network. Continue to receive post-program support and scaling resources.

Access continuous hiring support, PR contacts, and enterprise scaling partnerships.

Celebrate your immense success and officially start your rapid journey towards Series A funding.
